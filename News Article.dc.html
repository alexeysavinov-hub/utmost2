Mamboo Entertainment Surpasses $55 Million in Lifetime Revenue and Confirms Sustained Profitability

Global mobile publisher grows steadily through app store publishing, creative services, and service revenue.

Abu Dhabi, UAE – January 29, 2026 – Mamboo Entertainment (part of Utmost Games), a global mobile games publisher and developer, today announced a major financial milestone: the company’s lifetime revenue has exceeded $55 million.

Mamboo has remained profitable for the past three years, demonstrating consistent YoY growth of 20–35% across key metrics such as revenue, profit, and user base.

The company has previously achieved over 1 billion worldwide installs, published more than 50 games, and continues to expand its portfolio, including successful titles such as TowerLands. Every six months, Mamboo launches a global hit game that earns over $10 million in net profit over its lifetime.

The company’s growth is driven by effective business diversification:

  • Publishing leading developers’ games on alternative app stores (Samsung Galaxy Store, Huawei AppGallery, Amazon Appstore, and others), where Mamboo generates a significant share of installs and revenue for partners.
  • Service revenue from expertise in distribution, monetization, and project scaling.
  • Creative production, delivering high-converting creatives and marketing assets for mobile games.

“It's exciting to see our portfolio companies driving real growth and success thanks to their creative services, with Mamboo as a standout example. We continue to support our partners in reaching their full potential,” said Vladimir Nikolsky, CEO of Utmost Games.

“Reaching $55 million in lifetime revenue after three profitable years is a big moment for our team,” said Artur Beresnev, CEO of Mamboo Entertainment. “We’re thrilled to keep growing and support the mobile gaming community.”

Mamboo Entertainment has worked on creative production for a broad range of mobile games, both in-house and with partners. The portfolio includes titles such as Worm Out, Smash Legends, Sword Ninja, Forest Island, Stone Age Survival, Idle Outpost, Magic Tiles 3, and many more.

About Mamboo Entertainment

Founded in 2020 in Abu Dhabi with development operations in Warsaw, Mamboo Entertainment is a global mobile game publisher and developer. The company specializes in launching high-performing casual and midcore titles across both mainstream and alternative platforms. Mamboo works with studios worldwide to deliver innovative, scalable, and monetizable game experiences for a global audience.

TOP APP GAMES16 OCT 2025

MMA Star Alexander Volkov Joins Ludus: Merge Arena as a Playable Character Ahead of UFC 321 Bout

LIMASSOL, Cyprus — October 16, 2025 — Top App Games is excited to announce a special in-game collaboration between Ludus: Merge Arena and legendary MMA fighter Alexander Volkov. The reveal comes just days before he enters the octagon at UFC 321. The new character will officially launch in-game on October 22.

As part of the event, Volkov appears in Ludus as a fully playable, limited-time hero card, designed with input from the athlete himself. The character features a fiery visual frame, a glowing crown, and a powerful astral buff active for 100 days, dramatically boosting his performance on the battlefield.

Volkov’s in-game version also includes a signature tactical ability that reflects his real-life combat style: a balanced mix of high-impact strikes and endurance. The design positions him as one of the most formidable fighters in Ludus: Merge Arena.

The campaign will start on October 22 at 08:00 GMT and run for 5 days and 16 hours, ending on October 27 at 24:00 GMT.

This collaboration coincides with Volkov’s upcoming appearance at UFC 321, where he’s set to enter the octagon once again. While fans will watch him fight in Abu Dhabi, they’ll also be able to play as him in Ludus, turning October into a double feature for Volkov fans.

“Seeing my digital self in Ludus: Merge Arena is so exciting,” said Alexander Volkov. “The developers did an incredible job – my character really feels like me, from the way I move to the way I think. I can’t wait for players to jump in and start battling.”

“Alexander Volkov brings real fighting spirit to Ludus: Merge Arena,” said Vladimir Markov, CEO of Top App Games. “By combining MMA power with Ludus strategy, players can step into the arena and experience the adrenaline and focus that fighters bring to the octagon.”

To mark the collaboration, Volkov is launching the #LudusXDrago challenge, a global call for fans to show how they “train with Ludus.” Participants can post short workout videos while playing Ludus: Merge Arena on TikTok, Reels, or Shorts using the hashtag #LudusXDrago by October 20.

Volkov will personally pick the winners: a trip to Abu Dhabi and UFC 321 ticket, an autographed glove, and 50 in-game hero rewards are up for grabs.

Follow Ludus: Merge Arena to stay updated on the collaboration on X (Twitter), YouTube, TikTok, Discord, Instagram, and Facebook.

Founded in 2024, the company combines real-time PvP dynamics with card-based RPG elements. The success of Ludus has propelled Top App Games toward more ambitious projects and global expansion.

Ludus: Merge Arena Battle PvP is a realtime PvP card game where you build powerful card decks, merge heroes, and battle players worldwide in tactical PvP arenas. Unlock and upgrade unique summoners, deploy fantasy warriors, and master merging mechanics to dominate the battlefield.

About Alexander Volkov

Alexander “Drago” Volkov is a professional mixed martial artist competing in the UFC heavyweight division. A professional since 2009, Volkov previously fought for M-1 Global and Bellator MMA, where he captured heavyweight championship titles in both promotions. As of October 7, 2025, he is #2 in the UFC heavyweight rankings.

Ludus: Merge Arena Introduces Warcrow Heroes in Crossover Event

Cyprus, September 26 — Mobile game developer Top App Games has announced a collaboration between its popular mobile strategy title Ludus: Merge Arena and Warcrow, the fantasy miniature wargame created by Corvus Belli. For the first time, two legendary heroes from the Warcrow universe will join the Ludus: Merge Arena roster in a limited-time Astral Rift event, running from September 25 till October 16.

During the campaign, Ludus players will have the chance to unlock two Warcrow characters (the leaders of two opposing factions):

  • Verena of Aurtigard, the High Priestess, available as a reward in the event. Verena is a powerful defensive support mage, ideal for keeping the frontline alive and mitigating damage.
  • Uruk, Mage and Warrior, unlockable exclusively through a special purchase. Uruk is an elite predator, capable of eliminating enemies with unpredictable movements, leading from the front without fear (thanks to self-healing and tanking), and controlling the pace of battle through intimidation and reactive attacks.

Warcrow, set in the fantastical world of Lindwurm, is Corvus Belli’s latest tabletop wargame. Players command warriors, creatures, and wizards in skirmishes shaped by faction strategy and lore. The recent release of The Song of the Dormant battle pack – featuring the new factions Feudom and the Scions of Yaldabaoth – has brought a surge of new players, while the competitive scene launches this fall.

The Astral Rift is a recurring event in Ludus, where players compete in PvP battles to earn two key resources: Compasses and Spheres. Compasses are used to “tear open the rift” and unlock rewards, which may include exclusive legendary heroes such as Ludusman or Alice. Spheres, meanwhile, advance players along the Astral Pass, and grant additional bonuses. The event usually lasts for five days and is available to all players who have reached Arena 3 or higher.

The collaboration also extends to community events. The Top App Games team has prepared social media giveaways of official Warcrow board game boxes. Additional in-game news updates will keep players connected to the event throughout its duration.

“Warcrow brings a rich fantasy universe with iconic characters, and collaborations like this show how Ludus: Merge Arena and mobile games in general can connect different worlds and expand the possibilities of play,” said Vladimir Markov, CEO of Top App Games.

“At Corvus Belli, we’re excited to bring worlds together. Verena and Uruk are stepping into both the tabletop battlefield and the digital realm, giving fans twice the opportunities to enjoy their stories,” said Max Lattanzi, Head of Marketing for Corvus Belli. “Our partnership unites the best of wargaming and mobile fantasy gaming. These characters will join players across both worlds for epic adventures. When strategy meets fantasy across tabletop and mobile, magic happens – and Verena and Uruk are here to lead the charge in both games.”

TOP APP GAMES12 MAR 2025

Ludus: Merge Arena Reaches 5 Million Players and Announces Clan Wars Major Update

March 12th, 2025 – The popular mobile strategy RPG Ludus: Merge Arena has surpassed 5 million players worldwide and generated close to $3 million in monthly revenue since its launch. Publisher Top App Games Ltd. has also introduced a major game update: Clan Wars, an expansion of the clan mechanics set to release at the end of March.

The game now has three new tabs: Clan Shop, Clan Battle Pass, and Clan War. The primary source of points is Clan War, which occurs twice a month and lasts for eight days—five days of active competition followed by a three-day wrap-up phase.

In the Clan Shop, players can spend a special currency earned through Clan Wars. Exclusive items and special offers are available only during these events.

Clan Wars consist of six clans competing in a group, with each clan striving to earn the highest number of points by using flags for attacks. All players start with two flags, while premium subscribers receive an additional flag. Players can also obtain 1-2 extra flags by purchasing the Battle Pass, which allows premium users to use up to five flags in total.

Flags can be used in two ways: standard PvP battles, where players face random opponents to earn Clan War points, or Assault—a unique mode where players attack enemy base buildings.

At the end of each Clan War, the top 100 clans receive exclusive rewards, including cosmetic upgrades and a golden clan name, which remains visible until the next war.

“The Clan Wars update introduces competitive, clan-based gameplay with new mechanics, rewards, and strategic depth. These features were highly anticipated by our audience,” says Vladimir Markov, CEO of Top App Games. “In addition to Clan Wars, we plan to roll out regular content updates featuring new characters, as well as special in-game events.”

Top App Games Ltd. continues to attract investments from various sources. Last year, the studio secured funding from Vladimir Nikolsky (Utmost Games), along with other prominent investors.

“Ludus: Merge Arena continues to grow, expanding its player base and increasing revenue,” says Vladimir Nikolsky. “With Clan Wars, we’re excited to bring even more interest to the game.”

About Ludus: Merge Arena

Ludus: Merge Arena is a real-time PvP strategy game where players collect, merge, and upgrade heroes with unique abilities, creating powerful decks to defeat opponents worldwide. Seasonal tournaments, battle passes, and event rewards ensure a competitive yet welcoming experience for both casual and hardcore players.

About Top App Games Ltd.

Based in Cyprus, Top App Games Ltd. specializes in mobile game development and publishing, with a strong focus on strategy-based titles like Ludus: Merge Arena. Founded in 2024, the company blends real-time PvP dynamics with card-based RPG mechanics. The success of Ludus has fueled the studio’s ambition to expand into new markets and establish a stronger global presence.

Digital Vortex Entertainment: A New Era of Game Publishing

Digital Vortex Entertainment is proud to announce its official launch as an independent publisher of PC and console games. The team is dedicated to sharing its passion for games with a discerning audience.
